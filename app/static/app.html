            Red flags to remember

            • Anyone asking for an OTP, UPI PIN, CVV or password
            • Pressure: “within 24 hours”, “account will be blocked”
            • Brand names spelled oddly: arnazon, paypa1, g00gle
            • Links that aren't the company's real domain
            • Fees to release a prize, refund, parcel or job
            • “Police / CBI / customs” on a video call (“digital arrest”)
            • Requests to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer or an APK
            • “Keep this secret, don't tell your family or bank”
