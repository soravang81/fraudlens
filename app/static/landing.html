Features

Everything you need to check a message with confidence

Explained verdicts

A 0–100 risk score, the scam type, and every warning sign with the exact words that triggered it.

Message marked up

Your message is shown with risky phrases, reassuring lines and links highlighted, so you learn the pattern.

Link intelligence

Look-alike and disguised brands (arnazon, paypa1), threat feeds, site reputation and optional live redirect checks.

Bulk check

Check up to 20 messages or links at once and export the results as CSV.

Learn & quiz

A scam library with what to do for each type, plus a “spot the scam” quiz to train your instincts.

Private by design

Analysed in memory, never stored or logged. Your history and dashboard live only in your browser.

How it works

Four independent checks, one clear answer

  1. 1

    Normalise

    Strip hidden characters and undo disguises like “bl0cked”, “K.Y.C” and hxxp://.

  2. 2

    Read the wording

    20+ Indian-context scam patterns, disguised brand names and email-header checks (SPF, DKIM, Reply-To).

  3. 3

    Two machine-learning models

    A language model and a URL model trained on 770,000 labelled examples, tuned on data kept apart from testing.

  4. 4

    Inspect every link

    Threat feeds (OpenPhish, URLhaus), top-site reputation, look-alike domains, and optional live redirect and domain-age checks.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is my message stored anywhere?

No. It's analysed in memory and discarded. Server logs record only the risk level, score and time. Your history is kept in your own browser and you can switch it off.

How accurate is it?

Every number is measured on data never used for training and published on the Accuracy page, including every mistake on our hand-written test set. It is a strong second opinion, not a guarantee.

What should I do if I already paid or shared an OTP?

Call 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) immediately and report at cybercrime.gov.in. Then call your bank on its official number to block cards and UPI.

Can developers use it?

Yes. There's a JSON API (POST /api/analyze and POST /api/batch). See the API docs.
